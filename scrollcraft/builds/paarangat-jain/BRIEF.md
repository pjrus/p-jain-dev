## Grammar Choice & Why the Other Seven Lost

- **Chosen grammar:** **Gallery / catalog (`uniqueness.md` §2.6)**
- Why the others lost:
  1. *Filmic one-shot (§2.1):* Forbids navigation jumps and relies on continuous video; recruiters doing a 60-second skim need direct jump links and verifiable screenshots.
  2. *Chaptered editorial (§2.2):* Forbids media above the fold; hiring managers expect visual proof of real work immediately.
  3. *Live surface (§2.3):* Assumes one single SaaS application rather than a portfolio spanning web, mobile, and physical assistive hardware.
  4. *Continuous world (§2.4):* Demands continuous video flight legs; wrong for multi-project engineering proof.
  5. *Typographic poster (§2.5):* Bans product photography/screenshots, discarding Paarangat's strongest verifiable evidence.
  6. *Split stage (§2.7):* Built for binary before/after comparisons rather than a body of work.
  7. *Rhythmic cutlist (§2.8):* Fast-cut hype cadence clashes with calm, accessible engineering craft.
